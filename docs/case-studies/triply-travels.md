## Full case study narrative

### Building Triply Travels from the Ground Up

Launching a travel company involves more than creating an attractive website. The business must have a formal structure, appropriate operating permissions, financial arrangements and digital systems that can support both travelers and agents.

**Triply Travels Nigeria Limited** engaged iWORKS GLOBAL for an integrated set of business establishment and technology services. Our work supported the company from its incorporation stage through the development of its online presence and dedicated account portals.

### The Challenge: One Business, Several Foundations to Build

Triply Travels needed a coordinated approach to getting its travel business ready to operate. Company formation, regulatory licensing, business banking and digital customer experiences all had to be addressed.

Its website also needed to serve different audiences: travelers looking for assistance and travel services, and agents working with the business. A general-purpose website alone would not cover those different user journeys.

### What iWORKS GLOBAL Delivered

**Company formation.** We supported the formal incorporation of Triply Travels, helping establish the legal business entity underpinning its operations.

**Licensing and business setup.** We assisted the business in obtaining its required operating licences and coordinated the business bank-account opening process. This work helped connect the company's corporate setup with the practical requirements of conducting business. Specific licensing authorities and financial institutions have intentionally been omitted pending client-approved details.

**Travel website design and development.** We built the company's public-facing travel website, giving it an online destination for presenting travel services, destinations and customer enquiry or registration pathways. The current public site describes services including flights, hotels, visa assistance, travel insurance and tours.

**Dedicated client portal.** The platform includes a customer-facing account experience, providing a dedicated entry point for clients rather than relying on a brochure-only website.

**Dedicated agent portal.** We also developed a separate portal for agents, reflecting the distinct account and operational needs of the agency's service model.

### The Outcome: A Coordinated Business and Digital Foundation

The result was an integrated delivery spanning formal company establishment, licensing support, business banking arrangements and a purpose-built travel website with separate client and agent experiences.

Rather than treating the corporate setup and website as disconnected projects, iWORKS GLOBAL helped bring these foundational services together so Triply Travels could establish its business presence and serve its audiences through a unified digital platform.

### What This Project Demonstrates

Triply Travels shows how iWORKS GLOBAL combines business-development services with practical software delivery. We can support a company through essential formation requirements, then build the customer and internal digital experiences it needs as it grows.
