## Full case study narrative

### Supporting Restoration LLC's Digital Presence Since 2023

For a healthcare practice, a website is often the first place someone goes to understand available services, learn how to reach the practice and decide whether to make an enquiry.

**Restoration LLC**, a psychiatric and mental-health care practice based in Catonsville, Maryland, needed a professional digital presence to communicate its services clearly and make essential information accessible to visitors.

iWORKS GLOBAL has worked with Restoration LLC since **2023**, delivering **website design, hosting and ongoing website management** as part of a continuing digital-support relationship.

### The Challenge: A Professional, Easy-to-Navigate Healthcare Website

Mental-health services require clear, approachable information. Visitors may be exploring treatment options, reviewing insurance information or looking for a way to contact a provider.

The website needed to present the practice professionally while making its services and contact pathways easy to understand. Reliable hosting and ongoing website management were also important to supporting the practice's online presence beyond the initial launch.

### What iWORKS GLOBAL Delivered

**Website design and development.** We designed and developed Restoration LLC's web presence with an emphasis on a professional presentation and straightforward navigation. The site introduces the practice's approach and organises information about the conditions and services it addresses.

**Website hosting.** iWORKS GLOBAL provides the hosting environment for the website, supporting its continued online availability. We have maintained this hosting relationship since 2023.

**Ongoing website management.** Our relationship continues beyond the initial website build. We provide ongoing website management to support the practice's digital presence as its website and operational needs evolve.

**Patient-facing information architecture.** The public website makes core information—including treatment areas, accepted insurance providers, business hours and contact details—available to visitors. It also provides a way to begin an appointment enquiry.

### The Outcome: A Continuing Digital Partnership

Restoration LLC has a dedicated website that explains its services and helps prospective visitors find important practice information and contact options.

The project reflects more than a one-time website delivery: **iWORKS GLOBAL has remained involved in the design, hosting and management of Restoration LLC's website since 2023.**

That continuing relationship illustrates our approach to digital services—building websites that serve a real business purpose, then remaining available to manage and support their online presence.

### What This Project Demonstrates

From business websites to specialised professional-service platforms, iWORKS GLOBAL delivers practical design, reliable hosting arrangements and ongoing website management tailored to a client's needs.
