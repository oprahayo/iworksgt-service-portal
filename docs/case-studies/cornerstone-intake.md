## Published case study narrative

### Designing a Website for Compassionate Senior-Care Support

For families who cannot always be physically present, finding trustworthy assistance for older relatives is an important decision. Cornerstonetl, LLC works to support seniors with daily tasks and practical care needs, helping families learn about available assistance.

iWORKS GLOBAL was engaged to design Cornerstonetl's website and help the organisation present its services through a professional digital presence.

### The Challenge

Senior-care organisations need to communicate in a way that is clear, respectful and easy for families to understand. Cornerstonetl needed a website that explained the kind of day-to-day assistance it offers, supported its professional identity and gave prospective clients a place to learn about the organisation.

A website also needs technical foundations beyond design. Domain services, encrypted web connections, hosting and ongoing maintenance arrangements all contribute to that foundation.

### What iWORKS GLOBAL Delivered

**Website design and development.** We designed a dedicated website for Cornerstonetl, LLC, with messaging focused on its compassionate approach to senior assistance and practical daily-living support.

**Domain, website hosting and SSL.** In addition to the website, our documented service relationship includes domain services, hosting and SSL provision.

**Website maintenance.** Our records also show an annual professional website-maintenance renewal in 2025, demonstrating support beyond the initial design engagement.

### The Outcome

Cornerstonetl gained a dedicated web presence to explain its senior-care services and present its approach to supporting families and older adults. The digital work was backed by documented hosting, domain, SSL and maintenance services.

The project reflects iWORKS GLOBAL's ability to combine website design with the infrastructure and technical support needed to sustain a client's digital presence.

### Our Approach

We deliver more than standalone websites. We help service organisations explain what they do clearly and support the technical foundations behind their online presence.
