## Visual direction

- **Simplicity first**: one proposition or action per screen, generous negative space, large headline, short supportive caption only if needed. Never fill every corner with content.
- Primary orange: **#FF6600**. Pair with charcoal/near-black and warm-white backgrounds. Strong orange/white and orange/charcoal contrast; alternate light and dark scenes deliberately.
- Bold clean sans-serif, large uppercase headlines, readable at mobile size. Avoid large paragraphs, tightly packed lists and tiny caption text.
- Official small logo aligned consistently in the top safe area; prominent final brand lockup.
- Stylized abstract brand motif, concentric arcs, simple line drawings, checkmark/shield, short progress UI, and authentic iWorks website/product screenshots. Never misrepresent mockups as production capabilities.
- Clean typography with 1–3 words per line; e.g. BUSINESS. MADE EASY. / REGISTER. / PROTECT. / BUILD ONLINE. / GROW. / ONE PORTAL.

## Motion language

- Real time-based animation (not stationary slides): kinetic text with staggered entrances, easing, arc rotation, scale/zoom, horizontal/vertical motion, animated lines and card/progress elements.
- Controlled camera-like pans/push-ins, rhythm-synced cuts, purposeful transitions. High visual energy without clutter, spinning everything constantly or disorienting motion.
- Hold clear headline long enough to read. Respect 9:16 safe areas for Reels/Shorts/Status (keep critical text away from edges/overlays).
- Background instrumental original/licensed audio only. No compulsory voice-over. Ensure motion remains understandable without sound.
